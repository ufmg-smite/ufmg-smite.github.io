About

We are SMITE (SMT-based Methods and Interactive Theorem Proving Environment), a research group at the Department of Computer Science of UFMG (DCC/UFMG). We work on formal methods, software verification, and SMT solving, with an emphasis on automated reasoning, proof systems, and scalable verification techniques.

We develop tools and techniques for SMT-based reasoning, including Carcara, an efficient proof checker and elaborator for SMT proofs. Our group is led by Prof. Haniel Barbosa, and our research spans program verification, hardware verification, and the synthesis and certification of software systems.
